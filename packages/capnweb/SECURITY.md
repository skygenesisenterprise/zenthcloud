## Reporting a Vulnerability

* <https://github.com/skygenesisenterprise/zenthcloud>
  * All Zenthcloud products are in scope for reporting. If you submit a valid report on
    bounty-eligible assets through our disclosure program, we will transfer your report to our
    private bug bounty program and invite you as a participant.
* `mailto:security@zenthcloud.com`
  * If you'd like to encrypt your message, please do so within the body of the message. Our email
    system doesn't handle PGP-MIME well.
  * <https://github.com/skygenesisenterprise/zenthcloud>

All abuse reports should be submitted to our Trust & Safety team through our dedicated page:
<https://github.com/skygenesisenterprise/zenthcloud>
